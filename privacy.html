2. What this policy covers

This policy covers personal information we handle when you:

  • visit the app or these pages
  • create an account, are invited to a company, or use the app
  • subscribe, pay or contact us

Builders who subscribe ("customers") also use the app to store information about their own clients and leads. For that information the builder decides what is collected and why. We handle it only on the builder's behalf, as set out in our Data Processing Agreement. See section 6.

3. Information we collect

Account and user information

  • Your name, email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider, and we can't see it.
  • The company you belong to, your role (owner, admin, consultant and so on) and your access settings.
  • Invitations you send or receive: the invitee's email address, role and status.

Company and billing information

  • Company details you enter: company name, ABN, address, phone, email, website, logo and brand colour.
  • Plan, seat limit, trial dates and subscription status.
  • Billing details collected by Stripe at checkout: billing name, email, address, ABN or tax ID, and payment card. Card numbers go straight to Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We keep Stripe's customer and subscription reference numbers so we can match your payments to your company.

Information you put into the app

  • Client and lead records: names, email addresses, phone numbers, enquiry date, build type, buyer type, budget, timing, pipeline stage, appointment and meeting dates, outcome (won or lost), lead source and notes.
  • Dwelling or lot details: street number and name, suburb, estate and notes.
  • Tasks, stage history (who moved a client between stages and when), and sales activity figures and targets.
  • Documents you upload against a client record, such as plans or quotes.
  • Messages you send to us through the app's support chat.

Website and advertising enquiries

A builder can connect its website forms, Google or Meta lead forms, or a tool such as Zapier to the app, so that new enquiries go straight into its pipeline. Those enquiries can include:

  • the person's first and last name, email address and phone number
  • what they're interested in, where they want to build (suburb or postcode), their journey stage and whether they have land
  • their comments, and the campaign, form or lead ID that the enquiry came from

Technical information

  • When you use the app, our hosting and database providers record standard technical logs. These include IP address, browser type, the date and time of the request and the page or service requested. They help keep the service secure and working.
  • We keep an audit log of actions taken by our own administrators.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app.

We don't ask for sensitive information such as health information or racial or ethnic origin. Please don't put it in notes or uploaded documents unless you need to and you have the person's consent.

4. How we collect it

We collect most information directly from you when you sign up, use the app or contact us. We also get information from Stripe about your payments and subscription status. Client and lead information comes from the builder's own users, or from forms the builder has connected to the app.

You can use our public pages without telling us who you are. You can't use the app without an account, because we need to know which company's data you're allowed to see.

5. How we use it

  • To provide the app: sign you in, keep each company's data separate, apply roles and permissions, and show your pipeline, reports and forecasts.
  • To manage subscriptions: start trials, take payments, send tax invoices, apply plan and seat limits, and limit access when payments fail.
  • To support you, answer questions and fix problems.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent misuse and investigate incidents.
  • To send service messages, such as account confirmation, password reset, and billing or important service notices.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including tax and record keeping.

We don't sell personal information. We don't use your clients' information for our own marketing, and we don't use it to build profiles or train models.

6. Your clients' information

When a builder stores information about its clients or leads in the app, the builder is responsible for that information. The builder must:

  • have a lawful basis to collect it
  • tell its clients how their information is handled, for example in the builder's own privacy policy
  • respond to its clients' requests for access or correction

We act as the builder's service provider. We use that information only to provide the app to the builder, as set out in the Data Processing Agreement. If a builder's client contacts us about their information, we will pass the request to the builder, or help the builder to deal with it.

Our main database and stored files are in Australia. As the table above shows, some providers are based in, or process information in, the United States and other countries. Before we use a provider, we take reasonable steps to make sure it protects personal information in a way that meets the APPs (APP 8). 9. Cookies and browser storage

The app doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies.

  • To keep you signed in, the app stores a sign-in token in your browser's local storage. If you clear it, you'll be signed out.
  • Stripe's checkout and billing pages set their own cookies, which help prevent fraud. Those pages are covered by Stripe's own privacy policy.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, change or disclosure. These include:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all traffic
  • encryption of stored data by our hosting provider
  • database rules that keep each company's records separate and limit what each role can see
  • private file storage for uploaded documents
  • password hashing
  • limited administrator access, with an audit log
  • regular security reviews of our database settings

No system is completely secure. Please use a strong, unique password and remove users who leave your company.

11. How long we keep it

  • We never delete company data automatically.
  • If a subscription ends or payment fails, the company's workspace becomes read-only, but its data stays in the app. That way you can come back, or export your data.
  • An owner can ask us to delete the company's data at any time. We will do so within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.
  • Our database provider keeps backups for a short time (currently up to 7 days), so deleted data disappears from the backups as they expire.
  • We keep billing and tax records for as long as Australian tax law requires, usually 5 years.

When we no longer need personal information, we take reasonable steps to destroy it or de-identify it.

12. Access and correction

You can see and update most of your own information in the app. Owners can update company details, and users can update client records.

You can also ask us for access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it. Email fred@spectrumcoach.com.au. We'll reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm who you are first.

If we refuse a request, we'll tell you why, as the law allows. If you're a client of a builder, please contact the builder first.

13. Direct marketing

We may send account owners occasional news about the app or Spectrum Coach's coaching services. Every marketing email has a way to unsubscribe, or you can email us. We never use your clients' or leads' information to market to them.

14. Data breaches

We follow the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme in Part IIIC of the Privacy Act. If we suspect a data breach, we will act quickly to contain it and assess it.

If a breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell the affected people and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as the Act requires. If a breach affects a builder's client information, we will tell the builder promptly, as set out in the DPA.

15. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your personal information, please email fred@spectrumcoach.com.au. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 7 days and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the OAIC at www.oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date. If we make a significant change, we'll tell account owners by email or in the app.
